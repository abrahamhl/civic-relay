## A. Horizon coordinator — English
**Subject:** Horizon Cluster 3 (SSRI-04 / DRS-03) — opt-in offline emergency coordination research demonstrator

Hello,

I am developing **OEP — Offline Emergency Protocol**, an open-source research prototype investigating permission-aware coordination and store-and-forward messaging across existing networks during infrastructure failure. This is not a new satellite operator or a live 112 service.

The current evidence comprises a reproducible 20–100 virtual-device simulator, a 3D building-collapse exercise, automated security/safety scenario tests, and an Android two-phone research APK that has compiled successfully in CI. **No physical offline delivery metrics or institutional authorisation are claimed yet.**

I am looking to contribute a measurable work package to an experienced Cluster 3 consortium, especially **HORIZON-CL3-2026-01-SSRI-04** or **DRS-03** (deadline: 5 November 2026). Would your team be open to a short discussion on expected outcomes, eligible consortium roles and real-world testing with emergency professionals?
